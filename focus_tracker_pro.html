About Focus Session Tracker

What is this app?

Focus Session Tracker is a browser-based productivity tool. Everything runs locally — no accounts, no servers, no data leaves your device.

  • Set a task and choose a session length (preset or custom in seconds, minutes, or hours)
  • Live countdown and progress bar keep you anchored during the session
  • Rate productivity (1–5 stars) and add notes after each session
  • History, focus-over-time graph, and leaderboard stored via localStorage
  • Multiple users can share the same browser — each user's data is stored separately
  • Customisable end-of-session alert sound — choose from 6 built-in synthesized tones or silence

The Pomodoro Technique

Created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s in Rome. Struggling to focus as a student, he grabbed a tomato-shaped kitchen timer (pomodoro = tomato) and committed to 25 minutes of uninterrupted work. He refined it into a full system, published in 2006.

The cycle

  • Work with full focus for 25 minutes (one Pomodoro)
  • Take a 5-minute short break
  • After 4 Pomodoros, take a 15–30 minute long break

Why it works

  • Beats procrastination — 25 minutes lowers the mental barrier to starting
  • Reduces fatigue — structured breaks prevent burnout
  • Improves focus — the running timer crowds out distractions
  • Builds self-awareness — tracking reveals how long tasks actually take
  • Creates momentum — each completed Pomodoro is a small win

Session lengths

  • 15 min — quick sprints, email triage
  • 25 min — classic Pomodoro, best for most work
  • 45 min — deep work blocks
  • Custom — any value in seconds, minutes, or hours
