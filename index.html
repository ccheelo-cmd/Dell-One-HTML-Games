How League Points Work:
Each game ranks players by its own metric (score games by points, timed games by accuracy then speed). Your finishing position in each game earns league points — 1st place = 25 pts, 2nd = 18, 3rd = 15, and so on. The totals below sum your league points across all games, creating one fair ranking even though the games score differently.
